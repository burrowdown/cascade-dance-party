About us

Cascade Dance Party is a team that experiments with better event organizing models. CDP is (and probably always will be) a benevolent dictatorship headed by Ruth Kaplan and Danny Burrow.

CDP has some key differences to most dance events. We ask more of our attendees than most events do, and we know that extra friction can be frustrating—but if you are willing to put in a little extra care, we think you'll agree that it's worth it.

Our attendees enjoy our minimally programmed, unstructured, highly connected events where our invitation policy means that everyone they meet is a friend-of-a-friend-of-a-friend. We strive to create an atmosphere where we can all take responsibility for ourselves and each other, be vulnerable, and safely make mistakes.

Our values

Our core values, some of which are in conflict, are:

  • fair compensation of labor
  • physical & financial accessibility
  • leadership transparency
  • COVID-19 safety
  • inclusion of parents & children
  • having fun

The Events

Cascade Pool Party

Pool Party takes place every summer at the Lookout Arts Quarry (home of, in our opinion, the best swimming hole in the Northwest). We spend the weekend unwinding in and by the water, surrounded by trees, blackberry bushes, and naked hippies. At night we dance in the nearby circus arts warehouse, which has wood floors, high ceilings, and excellent vibes. Sometimes we even catch the Perseids, if you want to stay up late to stargaze!

Attendees camp out on Friday, Saturday, and Sunday nights, with breakfasts and dinners served on Saturday and Sunday and all other meals up to them. There are potable water spigots and electricity available in several places. Portapotties are located throughout the site, and there is one shower that may or may not have hot water.

Kids are an important part of the vibe of this event—parents are encouraged to bring their children! The more the merrier.

Cascade Slumber Party

Slumber Party takes place on Presidents Day Weekend at a cozy lodge in the woods, within an hour of Portland. We hear that there's hikes and stuff, but to be honest we love to stay inside and play board games all day and never change out of our pajamas. There are several separate spaces in the lodge so you can often choose your level of boisterousness. At any moment there might be a quiet crafting circle, a D&D game, a sing-along in the sauna, a Smash Bros tournament, a dance practica, or a game of Catan to choose from.

Space is limited, and there's usually around 40-50 attendees. Most people sleep in bunk rooms of 4-10 people, though anyone brave enough is welcome to pitch a tent outside! Dancing takes place in the nearby pavilion and starts quite late, since with a smaller group we try to concentrate attendance.

We serve dinner only on Saturday, Sunday, and Monday nights (but not Friday), with all other food being handled yourself using the full kitchen.

This event is adults only, though we may someday have a separate satellite space for the parents and children in our community to stay.

What now?

If you're ready to accept your invitation, let your inviter know which communication channels to add you to. Once you are in one or more of the channels, you are officially a CDP invitee, and you can attend any event with a +1.

Discord

Discord is our default communication channel. It gets more frequent updates and hosts more robust conversations between attendees and organizers. Plus you can control your notifications! We recommend this if you are a Discord user!

Facebook

We post the most important updates to Facebook, but we don't game the algorithm, so you can't expect to be notified. Once you're in the group, make sure you find the upcoming event for links to event information and registration.

Email

We email you about three times per event, and we keep the emails brief.

Extra Credit

We have been organizing these events for eight years, and part of why we still feel energized and excited to do so is because we love interacting with these events as attendees, not just as organizers. Some of the friction and some of the asks we make of you are in service of sharing the load of responsibility so we can spend time with you.

How can you support us in engaging in our events as participants?

  • Help each other first before relying on us
  • Have reasonable expectations for everyone (us, yourself, each other)
  • Read the One True Email, answer the Gold Star Quiz
  • Ask us to dance!
